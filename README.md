# Dia + Notion + Claude Setup

One prompt that sets up a Mac with:

- **Dia** browser and the **Notion** desktop app
- **Claude in Chrome** and **Notion Web Clipper** extensions in Dia
- **Pinned tabs** for your most-used sites (based on what you tell it *and* your browsing history)
- **Notion AI Meeting Notes** that pops up whenever you join a call and saves transcripts to a Meetings database in your Notion

## How to use it

1. Open Claude Code.
2. Paste this:

   > Follow the instructions in https://github.com/kuttipulli/dia-notion-setup/blob/main/SETUP.md to set up my computer.

3. Follow along. Claude Code does the work and stops whenever it needs you to click something, sign in, or approve a permission. It never asks for your passwords.

Takes about 15–20 minutes. Works best on a Mac running macOS 13 or newer.

## Privacy

Your browsing history is only read on your own computer to count which sites you visit most. Nothing is saved or uploaded, and you approve every tab before it's pinned.
